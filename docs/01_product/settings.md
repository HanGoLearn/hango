# HanGo Settings

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the personalization and configuration options available to HanGo users.

The purpose of settings is to adapt the learning experience according to the learner's goals, preferences, and needs.

---

# Core Philosophy

Settings should improve the learning experience.

They should not create unnecessary complexity.

The system should provide enough personalization to make learning effective while maintaining simplicity.

---

# User Profile Settings

The learner profile contains information used by HanGo to personalize the experience.

---

# 1. Learning Goal

## Purpose

Understand why the learner is studying Chinese.

Possible goals:

- Conversational fluency.
- HSK preparation.
- Travel.
- Professional communication.
- Academic purposes.
- Personal interest.

The AI Teacher uses this information to recommend activities.

---

# 2. Current Chinese Level

## Purpose

Determine the learner's starting point.

The learner can select:

- Beginner.
- Intermediate.
- Advanced.

Optional:

HSK level estimate.

Example:

HSK 3.

HSK 4.

HSK 5.

---

# 3. Native Language

## Purpose

Adapt explanations.

Supported examples:

- Spanish.
- English.
- Other languages.

The AI Teacher uses this preference when explaining concepts.

---

# 4. Explanation Language

## Purpose

Control how the AI Teacher explains concepts.

Options:

- Chinese only.
- Chinese + English.
- Chinese + Spanish.

Advanced learners may prefer more Chinese exposure.

---

# 5. Learning Preferences

The learner can customize their experience.

Examples:

Preferred focus:

- Speaking.
- Listening.
- Reading.
- Writing.
- Grammar.
- Vocabulary.

---

# 6. Study Schedule

## Purpose

Support consistency.

Settings:

- Daily study goal.
- Preferred study time.
- Weekly frequency.

Examples:

15 minutes daily.

30 minutes daily.

1 hour daily.

---

# 7. Audio Settings

The learner can customize listening experience.

Options:

- Playback speed.
- Audio difficulty.
- Voice preference.

Examples:

Slow.

Normal.

Native speed.

---

# 8. AI Teacher Preferences

The learner can customize interaction style.

Options:

Correction style:

- Immediate correction.
- Correction after conversation.
- Only important mistakes.

Explanation style:

- Short explanations.
- Detailed explanations.

Conversation style:

- Friendly.
- Professional.
- Strict teacher.

---

# 9. Learning Difficulty

The learner can choose the challenge level.

Options:

Adaptive:

AI automatically adjusts difficulty.

Manual:

User selects difficulty.

---

# 10. Review Preferences

Controls how HanGo reinforces previous knowledge.

Options:

Review frequency.

Vocabulary repetition.

Grammar review.

Speaking repetition.

---

# Privacy Settings

Users control:

- Data storage.
- Learning history.
- AI memory.
- Voice recordings.

The learner should always understand what information is used.

---

# Account Settings

Includes:

- Profile information.
- Password.
- Subscription.
- Notifications.
- Language preferences.

---

# Design Principles

Settings should be:

## Simple

Avoid overwhelming users with too many options.

---

## Useful

Every setting should improve personalization.

---

## Understandable

Users should clearly understand the effect of changing a setting.

---

# Future Possibilities

Future settings may include:

- Personal learning plans.
- Custom AI Teacher personalities.
- Cultural interests.
- Industry-specific vocabulary.

---

# Success Definition

Settings succeed when every learner feels that HanGo adapts naturally to their personal learning journey.
