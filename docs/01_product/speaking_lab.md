# Purpose

The Speaking Lab is the dedicated environment for developing Chinese speaking ability.

Its purpose is to transform passive knowledge into active communication skills through continuous speaking practice, real-time interaction, and personalized feedback.

The Speaking Lab helps learners move from understanding Chinese to using Chinese naturally.

---

# Core Philosophy

Speaking ability is developed through production.

Knowing vocabulary and grammar does not automatically create speaking ability.

Learners need opportunities to:

- Produce sentences.
- Express ideas.
- Respond spontaneously.
- Adapt to different situations.
- Build confidence.

The Speaking Lab exists to provide those opportunities.

---

# Main Objectives

The Speaking Lab helps learners improve:

- Pronunciation.
- Fluency.
- Speaking confidence.
- Sentence construction.
- Vocabulary usage.
- Grammar application.
- Natural expression.
- Conversational ability.

---

# Speaking Practice Modes

The Speaking Lab contains different practice experiences.

Each mode develops a different aspect of speaking ability.

---

# 1. Pronunciation Training

## Purpose

Improve pronunciation accuracy and confidence.

The learner practices:

- Individual sounds.
- Tones.
- Words.
- Sentences.
- Longer expressions.

---

## Feedback

The system evaluates:

- Tone accuracy.
- Pronunciation clarity.
- Rhythm.
- Fluency.

Feedback should be actionable.

Example:

Instead of:

"Your pronunciation is wrong."

The system provides:

"The third tone in 你好 needs a deeper falling-rising movement."

---

# 2. Shadowing Practice

## Purpose

Develop natural pronunciation and listening ability by imitating authentic Chinese speech.

The learner:

1. Listens to native audio.
2. Repeats the sentence.
3. Receives feedback.
4. Repeats until improvement.

---

## Difficulty Progression

Beginner:

- Short sentences.
- Slow speed.

Intermediate:

- Natural conversations.

Advanced:

- News.
- Presentations.
- Professional speech.

---

# 3. Conversation Practice

## Purpose

Develop spontaneous communication.

The learner interacts with AI in realistic scenarios.

Examples:

Daily situations:

- Restaurant.
- Shopping.
- Travel.
- Meeting people.

Professional situations:

- Interviews.
- Meetings.
- Negotiations.

Advanced situations:

- Discussions.
- Debates.
- Opinions.

---

# Conversation Rules

The AI should:

- Adapt to learner level.
- Encourage longer responses.
- Avoid excessive correction during conversation.
- Provide feedback after the interaction.

The goal is communication first.

---

# 4. Role Play

## Purpose

Simulate real-world situations.

The learner receives a role.

Examples:

Restaurant:

Learner:
Customer.

AI:
Waiter.

---

Job interview:

Learner:
Candidate.

AI:
Interviewer.

---

Business meeting:

Learner:
Employee.

AI:
Manager.

---

Role plays should become increasingly complex as the learner progresses.

---

# 5. Interview Practice

## Purpose

Develop formal speaking ability.

Examples:

- University interviews.
- Job interviews.
- Self-introduction.
- Professional questions.

The AI evaluates:

- Content.
- Vocabulary.
- Grammar.
- Fluency.
- Confidence.

---

# 6. Debate Practice

## Purpose

Develop advanced communication skills.

Debates are designed for upper-intermediate and advanced learners.

Examples:

Topics:

- Technology.
- Education.
- Economy.
- Society.
- Culture.

The learner practices:

- Expressing opinions.
- Supporting arguments.
- Responding to opposing views.

---

# 7. Presentation Practice

## Purpose

Develop structured speaking ability.

The learner practices:

- Organizing ideas.
- Explaining topics.
- Speaking for longer periods.

Examples:

- Presenting a project.
- Explaining a topic.
- Giving an opinion.

---

# Speaking Evaluation System

Speaking performance is evaluated through multiple dimensions.

---

## Pronunciation

Ability to produce understandable Chinese sounds and tones.

---

## Fluency

Ability to speak smoothly without excessive hesitation.

---

## Accuracy

Correct use of grammar and vocabulary.

---

## Complexity

Ability to use increasingly advanced structures.

---

## Communication Effectiveness

Ability to successfully express ideas.

---

# Connection With Competency System

Speaking Lab contributes to the learner's speaking profile.

Progress updates include:

- Speaking confidence.
- Pronunciation.
- Fluency.
- Conversational ability.
- Scenario competence.

---

# Connection With AI Teacher

The AI Teacher supports the Speaking Lab by:

- Explaining mistakes.
- Creating personalized exercises.
- Suggesting practice activities.
- Reviewing performance.

---

# User Experience Principles

The Speaking Lab should feel:

- Safe.
- Encouraging.
- Challenging.
- Realistic.

Learners should feel comfortable making mistakes because mistakes are part of improvement.

---

# Success Definition

The Speaking Lab succeeds when learners become comfortable using Chinese spontaneously in real situations.

The final objective is not perfect speech.

The final objective is confident communication.
