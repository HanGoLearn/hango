# HanGo Dashboard

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The Dashboard is the central home experience of HanGo.

Its purpose is to provide learners with a clear overview of their learning journey, current progress, recommended activities, and immediate next actions.

The Dashboard should help learners begin each study session with confidence and direction.

---

# Core Philosophy

The Dashboard should answer four questions:

1. Where am I in my learning journey?

2. What should I do next?

3. How am I improving?

4. How can I continue progressing?

The learner should never feel lost inside the platform.

---

# Main Dashboard Components

The Dashboard contains several key sections.

---

# 1. Daily Learning Overview

## Purpose

Provide a quick summary of the learner's current activity.

Information displayed:

- Current learning goal.
- Daily objective.
- Study streak.
- Recent activity.
- Recommended next action.

Example:

"Today's goal:
Complete Restaurant Scenario - Ordering Food"

---

# 2. Continue Learning

## Purpose

Allow learners to immediately continue from where they stopped.

The system displays:

- Current scenario.
- Current lesson.
- Progress percentage.
- Next activity.

Example:

Restaurant

Intermediate Level

Lesson 4:

Asking about ingredients

Continue →

---

# 3. AI Teacher Recommendation

## Purpose

Provide personalized guidance.

The AI Teacher analyzes learner data and suggests actions.

Examples:

"You are improving your speaking confidence. Try a conversation practice."

"Your grammar accuracy is improving. Review 把 construction before continuing."

"You have not practiced listening recently."

# 6. Quick Access Modules

The Dashboard provides direct access to:

- AI Teacher.
- Speaking Lab.

---

# 7. Recent Achievements

## Purpose

Recognize meaningful progress.

Achievements should represent competence.

Examples:

"Completed your first full Chinese conversation."

"Successfully handled a hotel check-in simulation."

"Wrote your first professional email in Chinese."

---

# 8. Review Recommendations

## Purpose

Support retention.

The system recommends previous content.

Examples:

"Review vocabulary from Restaurant Scenario."

"Practice the grammar structures you frequently confuse."

"Repeat this dialogue at natural speed."

---

# Personalization

The Dashboard changes depending on the learner.

Examples:

Beginner learner:

More guidance and structured recommendations.

Advanced learner:

More independent challenges and complex tasks.

Professional learner:

More business communication content.

---

# Dashboard Design Principles

The Dashboard should be:

## Simple

Avoid overwhelming users with excessive information.

---

## Action-Oriented

Every element should lead toward a meaningful learning action.

---

## Personal

The experience should feel unique to each learner.

---

## Motivating

Progress should be visible and encouraging.

---

# Information Priority

The Dashboard prioritizes:

1. Next learning action.

2. Current progress.

3. AI recommendations.

4. Long-term growth.

Secondary information should not distract from learning.
