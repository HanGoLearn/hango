Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The Writing Lab is the dedicated environment for developing Chinese writing ability.

Its purpose is to help learners transform their vocabulary and grammar knowledge into clear, natural, and effective written communication.

The Writing Lab develops the ability to express ideas through Chinese writing in real-world contexts.

---

# Core Philosophy

Writing is not only about grammatical correctness.

Good writing requires:

- Clear ideas.
- Appropriate vocabulary.
- Natural expressions.
- Logical organization.
- Cultural awareness.

HanGo Writing Lab focuses on developing communication through writing rather than simply correcting mistakes.

---

# Main Objectives

The Writing Lab helps learners improve:

- Sentence construction.
- Grammar accuracy.
- Vocabulary usage.
- Writing fluency.
- Text organization.
- Natural expression.
- Formal and informal writing styles.

---

# Writing Development Path

Writing ability develops progressively.

The learner moves through different levels of production.

---

# Level 1: Sentence Building

## Purpose

Develop the ability to create correct and natural sentences.

Activities:

- Sentence creation.
- Word ordering.
- Grammar application.
- Translation practice.
- Sentence expansion.

Example:

Basic:

我喜欢中国菜。

Advanced:

我特别喜欢中国南方的菜，因为不同地区的饮食文化非常丰富。

---

# Level 2: Short Messages

## Purpose

Develop practical daily writing.

Examples:

- Text messages.
- Social media messages.
- Short notes.
- Invitations.

Skills developed:

- Natural expressions.
- Appropriate tone.
- Everyday vocabulary.

---

# Level 3: Paragraph Writing

## Purpose

Develop structured written expression.

Learners practice:

- Introducing topics.
- Explaining ideas.
- Describing experiences.
- Comparing concepts.

---

# Level 4: Practical Writing

## Purpose

Prepare learners for real communication.

Examples:

- Emails.
- Requests.
- Applications.
- Reviews.
- Explanations.

The learner develops the ability to write for specific purposes.

---

# Level 5: Advanced Writing

## Purpose

Develop high-level written communication.

Examples:

- Essays.
- Opinions.
- Reports.
- Academic writing.

Skills:

- Argumentation.
- Organization.
- Formal vocabulary.
- Complex grammar.

---

# Writing Practice Modes

---

# 1. Free Writing

## Purpose

Allow learners to express ideas freely.

Examples:

"Describe your hometown."

"Explain your opinion about technology."

The AI evaluates the writing and provides feedback.

---

# 2. Guided Writing

## Purpose

Support learners who need structure.

The system provides:

- Topic.
- Required vocabulary.
- Grammar structures.
- Writing objectives.

---

# 3. Rewrite Practice

## Purpose

Improve existing writing.

The learner provides a text.

The AI helps improve:

- Grammar.
- Vocabulary.
- Naturalness.
- Style.

---

# 4. Translation Challenge

## Purpose

Develop active language production.

The learner translates ideas into Chinese.

The focus is not literal translation.

The focus is expressing the meaning naturally.

---

# 5. Professional Writing

## Purpose

Prepare learners for real professional communication.

Examples:

- Business emails.
- Reports.
- Presentations.
- Formal messages.

---

# AI Writing Feedback

The AI should analyze writing through multiple dimensions.

---

## Grammar Accuracy

Identifies incorrect structures and explains corrections.

---

## Vocabulary Usage

Evaluates:

- Word choice.
- Appropriateness.
- Variety.

---

## Naturalness

Determines whether the writing sounds like natural Chinese.

---

## Organization

Evaluates:

- Logical flow.
- Paragraph structure.
- Clarity.

---

## Style

Analyzes whether the writing matches the intended context.

Examples:

Casual.

Professional.

Academic.

---

# Correction Philosophy

Corrections should teach.

The AI should not simply replace the learner's writing with a perfect version.

The learner should understand:

- What was wrong.
- Why it was wrong.
- How to improve.

---

# Difficulty Progression

Writing difficulty increases through:

- Longer texts.
- More complex grammar.
- More abstract topics.
- More formal vocabulary.
- Greater independence.

---

The goal is not perfect academic writing.
