# HanGo Competency Framework

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines how HanGo measures Chinese proficiency and learner development.

The Competency Framework describes what learners are able to do at each stage of their journey.

---

# Core Philosophy

HanGo measures ability, not only knowledge.

A learner's proficiency is determined by their capacity to use Chinese effectively in real situations.

Knowing a grammar structure is different from being able to use it naturally.

Knowing vocabulary is different from being able to communicate ideas.

---

# Competency Model

HanGo evaluates competence through two dimensions:

1. Language Skills.

2. Communication Abilities.

---

# Dimension 1: Language Skills

These represent fundamental language abilities.

---

# Listening Competency

## Purpose

Measure the ability to understand spoken Chinese.

---

## Developing

The learner can understand:

- Slow conversations.
- Familiar topics.
- Common expressions.

---

## Independent

The learner can understand:

- Natural conversations.
- Different speaking speeds.
- Common real-life situations.

---

## Advanced

The learner can understand:

- Complex discussions.
- Abstract topics.
- Professional and academic speech.

---

# Speaking Competency

## Purpose

Measure the ability to express ideas orally.

---

## Developing

The learner can:

- Produce simple sentences.
- Answer basic questions.
- Participate with support.

---

## Independent

The learner can:

- Maintain conversations.
- Explain experiences.
- Express opinions.

---

## Advanced

The learner can:

- Discuss complex topics.
- Adapt communication style.
- Speak naturally in professional contexts.

---

# Reading Competency

## Purpose

Measure the ability to understand written Chinese.

---

## Developing

The learner understands:

- Short texts.
- Common messages.
- Basic information.

---

## Independent

The learner understands:

- Articles.
- Reports.
- Longer conversations.

---

## Advanced

The learner understands:

- Academic texts.
- Professional materials.
- Complex written content.

---

# Writing Competency

## Purpose

Measure the ability to express ideas in written Chinese.

---

## Developing

The learner can write:

- Sentences.
- Short messages.
- Basic descriptions.

---

## Independent

The learner can write:

- Paragraphs.
- Emails.
- Explanations.

---

## Advanced

The learner can write:

- Reports.
- Essays.
- Professional content.

---

# Dimension 2: Communication Abilities

These represent practical language use.

---

# Daily Communication

Ability to handle everyday situations.

Examples:

- Ordering food.
- Shopping.
- Making plans.
- Talking about personal life.

---

# Social Communication

Ability to interact naturally with others.

Examples:

- Building relationships.
- Expressing emotions.
- Sharing experiences.
- Understanding social expressions.

---

# Travel Communication

Ability to communicate while traveling.

Examples:

- Hotels.
- Transportation.
- Problems.
- Recommendations.

---

# Professional Communication

Ability to communicate in workplace environments.

Examples:

- Meetings.
- Presentations.
- Negotiations.
- Business messages.

---

# Academic Communication

Ability to participate in educational environments.

Examples:

- Presentations.
- Discussions.
- Research topics.
- Formal writing.

---

# Competency Levels

HanGo uses five mastery stages.

---

# Level 1: Exposure

The learner has encountered the concept.

The learner can recognize it but cannot reliably use it.

---

# Level 2: Practice

The learner can use the skill with guidance.

Mistakes are frequent.

---

# Level 3: Functional

The learner can use the skill independently.

Communication is possible despite mistakes.

---

# Level 4: Confident

The learner uses the skill naturally.

Mistakes are occasional.

---

# Level 5: Mastery

The learner can use the skill flexibly across different contexts.

The learner understands nuance and cultural differences.

---

# Scenario Competency

Every scenario receives its own competency evaluation.

Example:

Restaurant Scenario:

Beginner competency:

Can order basic food.

Intermediate competency:

Can discuss preferences and solve problems.

Advanced competency:

Can discuss culture, opinions, and social topics.

---

# HSK Relationship

HSK levels are used as a reference.

They help estimate:

- Vocabulary range.
- Grammar expectations.
- General difficulty.

However:

HSK level ≠ Complete communication ability.

HanGo combines HSK progression with real-world competency.

---

# AI Evaluation

- Learning behavior.
