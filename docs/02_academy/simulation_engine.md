# HanGo Simulation Engine

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The HanGo Simulation Engine is responsible for generating realistic, dynamic, and personalized communication experiences.

Unlike scripted exercises, simulations are open-ended interactions where learners must apply their Chinese knowledge to accomplish meaningful objectives.

The Simulation Engine represents the final stage of practical learning.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Real communication is unpredictable.

People do not follow scripts.

They interrupt.

They misunderstand.

They ask unexpected questions.

They change topics.

Therefore, HanGo should prepare learners for authentic communication rather than memorized conversations.

---

# Core Objective

Every simulation should answer one question:

"Can the learner successfully communicate in this situation?"

The objective is communication success rather than grammatical perfection.

---

# Dynamic Conversation

The AI controls the conversation naturally.

It should:

- Ask questions.
- React to learner answers.
- Change direction when appropriate.
- Clarify misunderstandings.
- Maintain realistic behavior.

The learner should never feel that they are speaking with a scripted chatbot.

# Adaptive Difficulty

The Simulation Engine continuously adjusts challenge based on learner performance.

Possible adjustments:

- Vocabulary complexity.
- Grammar complexity.
- Speaking speed.
- Number of unexpected events.
- Conversation length.

The goal is to keep learners challenged without overwhelming them.

# Design Principles

Every simulation should be:

- Realistic.
- Dynamic.
- Personalized.
- Educational.
- Challenging.
- Replayable.

Two simulations should rarely feel identical.

# Success Definition

The Simulation Engine succeeds when learners feel capable of handling real Chinese conversations outside the platform with confidence and adaptability.
