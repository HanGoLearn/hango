# Purpose

This document defines how HanGo creates and organizes learning scenarios.

Scenarios are the foundation of HanGo's communication-based learning methodology.

They transform language knowledge into practical communication ability.

---

# What Is a Scenario?

A Scenario is a realistic situation where a learner needs to use Chinese to achieve a specific objective.

A scenario contains:

- A context.
- A communication goal.
- Required language.
- Practice activities.
- A final mission.

---

# Scenario Philosophy

Language exists to accomplish things.

Therefore, learners should acquire Chinese through meaningful actions.

Example:

Traditional approach:

```
Grammar:
把 sentence structure

Vocabulary:
Travel words
```

HanGo approach:

```
Scenario:
Solving a problem at a hotel

Communication goal:
Explain a problem, request assistance, and negotiate a solution.

Required language:
把 structure + problem vocabulary
```

# Scenario Structure

Every scenario follows this structure:

```
Scenario

↓

Difficulty Level

↓

Communication Objectives

↓

Lessons

↓

Activities

↓

Mission

↓

Evaluation
```

---

# Scenario Components

---

# 1. Context

Defines the environment.

Includes:

- Location.
- Participants.
- Situation.
- Objective.

Example:

```
Location:

Restaurant in Beijing

Participants:

Customer and waiter

Situation:

Customer has a food allergy.

Objective:

Explain the problem and order safely.
```

---

# 2. Communication Goals

Every scenario must define what the learner can accomplish.

Goals should use action verbs.

Examples:

The learner can:

- Ask for information.
- Explain a problem.
- Give an opinion.
- Negotiate.
- Describe experiences.

---

# 3. Language Requirements

Each scenario defines the language needed.

Includes:

## Vocabulary

Words and expressions required.

## Grammar

Structures needed for communication.

## Functional Expressions

Natural phrases used by native speakers.

---

# 4. Lessons

A scenario is divided into multiple lessons.

Each lesson focuses on a specific communication objective.

Example:

Scenario:

Hotel Problem

Lessons:

1. Checking into a hotel.

2. Asking about facilities.

3. Reporting a problem.

4. Negotiating a solution.

---

# 5. Dialogue System

Each scenario includes realistic conversations.

Dialogues should contain:

- Natural expressions.
- Appropriate difficulty.
- Cultural context.
- Target vocabulary.
- Target grammar.

Dialogues should represent real communication, not textbook sentences.

---

# 6. Activities

Activities transform knowledge into ability.

Examples:

Listening:

Identify key information.

Speaking:

Role-play the situation.

Writing:

Send a message related to the scenario.

AI Practice:

Have a conversation with the AI Teacher.

---

# 7. Scenario Mission

The mission is the final challenge.

The learner must complete a realistic task.

Examples:

Restaurant:

"Order a complete meal and explain your preferences."

Hotel:

"Report a room problem and find a solution."

Business:

"Negotiate a meeting schedule."

---

# Scenario Evaluation

The learner is evaluated on:

## Communication Success

Was the objective achieved?

## Language Accuracy

Was Chinese used correctly?

## Vocabulary Usage

Was appropriate vocabulary used?

## Fluency

How naturally was Chinese produced?

## Adaptability

Could the learner respond to unexpected situations?

---

# Scenario Difficulty Progression

Difficulty increases through:

---

## Vocabulary

More specific and advanced words.

Example:

Food

↓

Nutrition

↓

Food industry

---

## Grammar

More complex structures.

Example:

Simple requests

↓

Explanations

↓

Arguments and opinions

---

## Conversation Complexity

From:

Short answers.

To:

Extended discussions.

---

## Independence

From:

Guided practice.

To:

Free communication.

# Scenario Reusability

Previously learned scenarios should return at higher levels.

Example:

Restaurant:

HSK 3:

Order food.

HSK 5:

Discuss preferences and culture.

HSK 8:

Debate food globalization.

The same context evolves with the learner.

---

# Scenario Creation Rules

Every new scenario must answer:

1. What real situation does this represent?

2. What can the learner do after completing it?

3. What Chinese is necessary?

4. How will the learner practice?

5. How will success be measured?

---

# Success Definition

The Scenario System succeeds when learners develop the ability to use Chinese naturally in situations that matter in real life.
