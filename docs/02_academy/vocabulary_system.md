# Purpose

This document defines how vocabulary is selected, introduced, reinforced, reviewed, and evaluated inside HanGo.

Vocabulary is not taught as isolated words. Instead, every word serves a communication purpose within real-life situations.

---

# Vocabulary Philosophy

Knowing a word does not mean knowing how to use it.

Learners should understand:

- Meaning.
- Pronunciation.
- Context.
- Usage.
- Register.
- Collocations.
- Common mistakes.

The ultimate goal is active vocabulary that learners can naturally use in conversation.

---

# Vocabulary Selection Principles

Every vocabulary item must satisfy at least one of the following conditions:

- Required to complete a communication objective.
- Frequently used by native speakers.
- Essential for understanding the dialogue.
- Necessary for future lessons.
- Required by the HSK curriculum.

Words should never be introduced simply to increase vocabulary size.

Examples:

---

## Functional Vocabulary

Words used to accomplish communicative tasks.

Examples:

建议

---

## Advanced Vocabulary

Lower-frequency words introduced in upper levels.

Used for:

- Professional communication.
- Academic discussions.
- Social issues.
- Abstract topics.

# Vocabulary Card Structure

Each vocabulary entry includes:

- Chinese characters.
- Simplified pronunciation (Pinyin).
- Meaning.
- Part of speech.
- Audio.
- Stroke order (optional).
- Example sentence.
- Common collocations.
- Synonyms.
- Antonyms (when appropriate).
- Related words.
- HSK reference.
- Frequency level.

---

# Contextual Learning

Vocabulary should first appear inside a dialogue or realistic context.

Learners should infer meaning before reading explanations whenever possible.

---

# Example Sentences

Every word includes multiple examples.

Examples should progress from:

Simple

↓

Natural

↓

Complex

↓

Professional or Academic (Advanced levels)

---

# Collocations

Learners should study words together with their most common combinations.

Example:

预约

↓

预约时间

预约医生

预约酒店

The objective is to help learners sound more natural.

---

# Word Families

Related vocabulary should be connected.

Example:

学习

↓

学生

学校

学习者

This helps learners recognize language patterns.

---

# Pronunciation

Every vocabulary item includes:

- Native-speed pronunciation.
- Slow pronunciation.
- Optional syllable-by-syllable playback.

Speaking activities should reinforce pronunciation immediately after learning new vocabulary.

---

# Controlled Practice

Vocabulary exercises include:

- Matching.
- Multiple choice.
- Fill in the blank.
- Sentence completion.
- Image association.
- Listening identification.

Exercises focus on recognition before production.

---

# Active Production

Learners must actively use vocabulary through:

- Speaking.
- Writing.
- AI conversations.
- Simulations.

Recognition alone is insufficient for mastery.

---

# Vocabulary Recycling

Previously learned vocabulary should appear continuously throughout future lessons.

Recycling occurs through:

- Dialogues.
- Reading passages.
- Listening activities.
- Speaking Lab.
- Writing Lab.
- Simulations.

Vocabulary should remain active throughout the learner's journey.

---

# AI Teacher Responsibilities

The AI Teacher should:

- Explain meaning.
- Provide synonyms when appropriate.
- Compare similar words.
- Generate additional examples.
- Correct incorrect usage.
- Recommend review when necessary.

---

# Vocabulary Mastery Levels

Every vocabulary item progresses through five stages.

## Level 1

Recognized.

The learner understands the word.

---

## Level 2

Understood.

The learner correctly interprets the word in context.

---

## Level 3

Controlled Usage.

The learner can use the word with guidance.

---

## Level 4

Independent Usage.

The learner naturally uses the word in conversation and writing.

---

## Level 5

Mastered.

The learner correctly uses the word across different contexts and understands nuance.

---

# Evaluation

Vocabulary evaluation measures:

- Recognition.
- Recall.
- Pronunciation.
- Contextual understanding.
- Active usage.
- Long-term retention.

The learner's vocabulary score should reflect practical communication ability rather than memorization.

---

# Success Definition

The Vocabulary System succeeds when learners can confidently understand and use vocabulary naturally in real-life communication.
