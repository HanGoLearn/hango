# HanGo Exercise System

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines how exercises are designed, generated, presented, and evaluated throughout HanGo.

Exercises transform passive understanding into active ability.

Every exercise must contribute directly to the learner's communication competency.

Exercises are not isolated tasks—they are building blocks toward real-world communication.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Exercises should progressively move learners from recognition to independent production.

The goal is not to memorize answers.

The goal is to confidently use Chinese in authentic situations.

Every exercise should answer one question:

"What communication skill is this helping the learner develop?"

---

# Exercise Progression

Every lesson follows the same progression.

```
Recognition

↓

Understanding

↓

Controlled Practice

↓

Guided Production

↓

Independent Production

↓

Real Communication
```

Each stage prepares learners for the next.

Exercises should never skip stages.

---

# Exercise Categories

HanGo includes six primary exercise categories.

---

## 1. Vocabulary Exercises

Purpose:

Build vocabulary recognition and active recall.

Examples:

- Matching words with meanings.
- Matching words with images.
- Listening and selecting the correct word.
- Completing collocations.
- Identifying synonyms.
- Identifying antonyms.
- Choosing the correct classifier (measure word).

Target competency:

Vocabulary acquisition.

---

## 2. Grammar Exercises

Purpose:

Develop accurate sentence construction.

Examples:

- Fill in the blanks.
- Sentence ordering.
- Error correction.
- Sentence transformation.
- Pattern substitution.
- Choosing the correct grammar structure.

Target competency:

Grammar accuracy.

---

## 3. Listening Exercises

Purpose:

Improve spoken Chinese comprehension.

Examples:

- Multiple choice.
- True or false.
- Identify speaker intention.
- Fill missing words.
- Sequence events.
- Select the correct response.

Target competency:

Listening comprehension.

---

## 4. Reading Exercises

Purpose:

Improve reading comprehension.

Examples:

- Main idea.
- Supporting details.
- Vocabulary in context.
- Paragraph ordering.
- Inference questions.
- Matching headings.

Target competency:

Reading comprehension.

---

## 5. Speaking Exercises

Purpose:

Develop spoken communication.

Examples:

- Sentence repetition.
- Shadowing.
- Role play.
- Guided dialogue.
- Picture description.
- Open conversation.

Target competency:

Speaking fluency.

---

## 6. Writing Exercises

Purpose:

Develop written communication.

Examples:

- Sentence creation.
- Dialogue completion.
- Short messages.
- Story continuation.
- Opinion writing.
- Situation response.

Target competency:

Writing ability.

---

# Exercise Difficulty

Every exercise is assigned one difficulty level.

## Beginner

Highly guided.

One correct answer.

Clear instructions.

---

## Intermediate

Some flexibility.

Multiple acceptable responses.

Limited guidance.

---

## Advanced

Open-ended production.

Communication-focused.

Realistic ambiguity.

---

# Exercise Generation

Exercises may be:

- Pre-designed.
- AI-generated.
- AI-personalized.
- AI-adapted.

Regardless of origin, every exercise must follow HanGo educational standards.

---

# Adaptive Exercises

The AI Teacher dynamically adjusts exercises according to learner performance.

Possible adaptations include:

- Easier vocabulary.
- Additional hints.
- More repetition.
- Increased challenge.
- Alternative examples.

Difficulty should evolve continuously.

---

# Feedback

Every exercise provides immediate feedback.

Feedback should include:

- Correct answer.
- Explanation.
- Why incorrect answers are wrong.
- Suggested review.
- Related grammar or vocabulary.

Feedback should always promote learning rather than simply indicating correctness.

---

# Hint System

Exercises support progressive hints.

Hint 1

General guidance.

---

Hint 2

Relevant grammar reminder.

---

Hint 3

Vocabulary suggestion.

---

Hint 4

Partial solution.

---

Hint 5

Complete explanation.

Hints should encourage independent thinking before revealing answers.

---

# Exercise Completion

An exercise is completed when the learner:

- Understands the objective.
- Successfully performs the required task.
- Receives feedback.
- Demonstrates comprehension.

Completion does not necessarily require a perfect score.

---

# AI Teacher Integration

The AI Teacher may:

- Generate additional exercises.
- Increase or decrease difficulty.
- Explain mistakes.
- Create similar examples.
- Extend exercises into conversations.

The AI should always understand which lesson and competency the learner is practicing.

---

# Accessibility

Exercises should support:

- Desktop.
- Tablet.
- Mobile.

Where appropriate, exercises should also support:

- Keyboard interaction.
- Touch interaction.
- Voice interaction.

---

# Exercise Design Principles

Every exercise should be:

- Short.
- Focused.
- Interactive.
- Contextual.
- Practical.
- Motivating.

Exercises should avoid unnecessary complexity unrelated to the communication objective.

---

# Success Definition

The Exercise System succeeds when learners progressively develop the confidence and ability to use Chinese accurately and naturally in real communication rather than simply completing academic tasks.
