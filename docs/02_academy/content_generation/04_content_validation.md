# Purpose

This document defines the validation process used to ensure that every piece of content published inside HanGo meets educational, linguistic, and user experience standards.

The objective is to guarantee that content quality remains consistent regardless of whether it is created manually, generated by AI, or adapted from external educational resources.

---

# Validation Philosophy

Generating content is not the same as creating quality content.

Every lesson, dialogue, exercise, mission, and learning object must pass validation before being available to learners.

The validation process protects:

- Educational quality.
- Language accuracy.
- Difficulty progression.
- Learning consistency.
- User experience.

---

# Validation Stages

Every content item passes through four validation stages.

```
1. Structural Validation

↓

2. Educational Validation

↓

3. Linguistic Validation

↓

4. Experience Validation

```

---

# Stage 1: Structural Validation

Purpose:

Confirm that the content follows the HanGo content model.

The system checks:

- Required fields exist.
- Correct format is used.
- Learning objects are properly connected.
- Metadata is complete.

Examples:

A lesson must contain:

- Learning objective.
- Scenario.
- Vocabulary.
- Grammar.
- Dialogue.
- Exercises.
- Mission.
- Assessment.

---

# Stage 2: Educational Validation

Purpose:

Verify that content actually contributes to learning.

The system evaluates:

## Learning Objective

Questions:

- Is the objective clear?
- Is it a communication ability?
- Can success be measured?

---

## Difficulty Alignment

Questions:

- Is the content appropriate for the learner level?
- Does it match HSK expectations?
- Does it connect with previous knowledge?

---

## Learning Progression

Questions:

- Does the content move from easier to harder?
- Are learners properly prepared before production?
- Does practice lead to communication?

---

## Competency Development

Questions:

- Which competency does this content improve?
- Does it contribute to the learner profile?

---

# Stage 3: Linguistic Validation

Purpose:

Ensure Chinese language accuracy and naturalness.

The system checks:

---

## Vocabulary

Verify:

- Correct meaning.
- Correct usage.
- Appropriate frequency.
- Natural examples.
- Correct collocations.

---

## Grammar

Verify:

- Correct structure.
- Correct explanation.
- Correct examples.
- Appropriate difficulty.

---

## Dialogue

Verify:

- Natural spoken Chinese.
- Realistic expressions.
- Appropriate politeness.
- Cultural accuracy.

---

## Pronunciation

Verify:

- Correct pinyin.
- Correct tones.
- Natural audio pronunciation.

---

# Stage 4: Experience Validation

Purpose:

Ensure the content creates a good learning experience.

The system evaluates:

## Engagement

Questions:

- Is the scenario interesting?
- Does the learner understand why it matters?

---

## Practical Value

Questions:

- Could the learner use this outside the platform?
- Does it solve a real communication need?

---

## Interaction Quality

Questions:

- Are exercises meaningful?
- Does the learner actively produce Chinese?
- Is feedback useful?

---

# Content Validation Checklist

Every lesson must satisfy:

## Structure

✓ Complete learning objects.

✓ Correct metadata.

✓ Correct difficulty.

---

## Learning

✓ Clear communication goal.

✓ Relevant vocabulary.

✓ Relevant grammar.

✓ Progressive practice.

✓ Meaningful mission.

---

## Language

✓ Natural Chinese.

✓ Correct grammar.

✓ Correct pronunciation.

✓ Appropriate cultural context.

---

## Experience

✓ Engaging scenario.

✓ Clear instructions.

✓ Useful feedback.

✓ Appropriate challenge.

---

# AI Content Validation

AI-generated content requires additional verification.

The system must check:

- Hallucinated information.
- Incorrect translations.
- Artificial expressions.
- Incorrect difficulty.
- Repeated patterns.
- Unnatural dialogues.

AI should generate content, but validation determines whether content is acceptable.

---

# Human Review

Certain content may require human review.

Examples:

- Advanced HSK 7-9 content.
- Professional Chinese.
- Cultural explanations.
- Sensitive topics.

Human review ensures expert-level quality.

---

# Validation Scores

Every content item receives quality scores.

Categories:

## Educational Score

Measures learning effectiveness.

---

## Language Score

Measures linguistic accuracy.

---

## Engagement Score

Measures learner experience.

---

## Overall Quality Score

Combines all categories.

Content below the required threshold must be improved before publication.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Published content is continuously improved using:

- Learner feedback.
- Completion rates.
- Common mistakes.
- Assessment results.
- AI recommendations.

Content quality should improve over time.

---

# Success Definition

The Content Validation System succeeds when every HanGo learning experience maintains consistent quality, accuracy, and educational value regardless of how the content was created.
