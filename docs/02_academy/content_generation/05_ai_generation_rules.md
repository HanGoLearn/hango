Status: Aspirational — not implemented (2026-09-05 audit)

---

# Purpose

This document defines the rules that AI systems must follow when generating educational content for HanGo.

The objective is to ensure that AI-generated materials maintain HanGo's educational philosophy, language quality, consistency, and learner-centered approach.

AI is a content creation assistant, not an independent curriculum designer.

---

# AI Generation Philosophy

AI should help scale content creation while preserving educational quality.

The AI must always prioritize:

- Communication ability.
- Natural Chinese usage.
- Progressive difficulty.
- Learner needs.
- Educational consistency.

The AI should never generate content only because it is grammatically correct.

Content must have a learning purpose.

---

# AI Content Generation Process

AI generation follows this sequence:

```
Learner Context

↓

Learning Objective

↓

Scenario

↓

Required Competencies

↓

Language Content

↓

Learning Objects

↓

Practice Activities

↓

Mission

↓

Assessment

```

AI must understand the complete context before creating content.

---

# Required AI Input

Before generating content, the AI should receive:

## Lesson Information

- Level.
- HSK reference.
- Scenario.
- Communication objective.
- Lesson position.
- Previous knowledge.

---

## Language Requirements

- Target vocabulary.
- Target grammar.
- Required expressions.
- Difficulty constraints.

---

## Learner Information

When generating personalized content:

- Current competency.
- Previous mistakes.
- Learning speed.
- Weak areas.
- Preferred practice style.

---

# General Generation Rules

AI-generated content must:

✓ Match the requested level.

✓ Use natural Chinese.

✓ Have a clear communication purpose.

✓ Avoid unnecessary complexity.

✓ Connect with previous learning.

✓ Encourage active production.

---

# Vocabulary Generation Rules

When generating vocabulary:

The AI must consider:

- Frequency of use.
- Real-life usefulness.
- Learner level.
- Scenario relevance.

The AI should provide:

- Chinese characters.
- Pinyin.
- Meaning.
- Part of speech.
- Examples.
- Common combinations.

The AI should avoid:

- Rare words without purpose.
- Direct translations that sound unnatural.
- Excessive vocabulary quantity.

---

# Grammar Generation Rules

When generating grammar explanations:

The AI must include:

- Meaning.
- Structure.
- Usage.
- Examples.
- Common mistakes.

The AI should:

- Compare similar grammar when needed.
- Avoid overly technical explanations for beginners.
- Encourage practical usage.

---

# Dialogue Generation Rules

AI-generated dialogues must:

## Be realistic

Characters should communicate naturally.

Avoid:

- Artificial textbook conversations.
- Repetitive patterns.
- Unnatural expressions.

---

## Match the level

Beginner:

- Short sentences.
- Common vocabulary.

Intermediate:

- Longer conversations.
- More flexible expressions.

Advanced:

- Abstract topics.
- Natural complexity.

---

## Include learning objectives

Target vocabulary and grammar should appear naturally.

They should not feel forced.

---

# Exercise Generation Rules

AI-generated exercises must:

- Practice a specific skill.
- Match lesson objectives.
- Progress from easier to harder.
- Include meaningful feedback.

The AI should avoid creating exercises that only test memorization.

---

# Speaking Activity Rules

Speaking activities should encourage:

- Active production.
- Natural responses.
- Personal expression.

Examples:

- Role play.
- Opinion questions.
- Situation responses.
- Conversation continuation.

The AI should evaluate communication, not only grammar accuracy.

---

# Writing Activity Rules

Writing tasks should:

- Have a clear purpose.
- Match learner level.
- Encourage original production.

Examples:

Beginner:

Write a short message.

Intermediate:

Describe an experience.

Advanced:

Explain an argument.

---

# Mission Generation Rules

AI missions must:

- Represent realistic situations.
- Have a clear objective.
- Require communication.
- Include possible variations.

The mission should feel like a real-life challenge, not an exercise.

---

# Quiz Generation Rules

AI quizzes must:

- Measure the intended skill.
- Avoid ambiguous questions.
- Provide explanations.
- Reinforce learning.

The AI should generate different versions to prevent memorization.

---

# Personalization Rules

When creating personalized content, AI should prioritize:

1. Learner weaknesses.

2. Recent mistakes.

3. Current learning goals.

4. Previous knowledge.

5. Appropriate challenge.

Personalization should improve learning efficiency, not simply increase difficulty.

---

# AI Limitations

AI must not:

- Create content above the learner level without permission.
- Introduce unexplained grammar.
- Invent cultural facts.
- Provide incorrect translations.
- Replace official curriculum decisions.

---

# Consistency Rules

All AI-generated content must follow:

- HanGo lesson structure.
- HanGo terminology.
- HanGo competency framework.
- HanGo assessment methodology.

---

# AI Review Loop

After generating content:

AI should evaluate:

- Is the objective clear?
- Is the content appropriate?
- Is the Chinese natural?
- Does the learner practice communication?
- Does the difficulty match?

If not, the content should be revised.

---

# Success Definition

The AI Generation Rules succeed when AI can efficiently create large amounts of high-quality Chinese learning content while maintaining HanGo's educational standards and communication-first philosophy.
