# Purpose

This document defines how HanGo evaluates learner performance, measures competency, and guides future learning.

Assessment is designed to measure communication ability rather than simple memorization.

The objective is to identify what learners can successfully do in Chinese and recommend the most effective next steps.

---

# Assessment Philosophy

Assessment is part of the learning process.

It should:

- Measure progress.
- Identify strengths.
- Detect weaknesses.
- Guide improvement.
- Motivate learners.

Assessments should never exist only to assign a score.

---

# Competency-Based Assessment

HanGo evaluates learners according to real communication abilities.

Every assessment answers questions such as:

- Can the learner complete the communication objective?
- Can the learner understand the situation?
- Can the learner express ideas clearly?
- Can the learner adapt when the conversation changes?

Communication success is always prioritized over perfect grammatical accuracy.

# Assessment Categories

HanGo evaluates the following competencies.

---

## Listening

Measures the learner's ability to understand spoken Chinese.

Evaluation criteria:

- General understanding.
- Specific details.
- Speaker intention.
- Natural-speed comprehension.

---

## Speaking

Measures spoken production.

Evaluation criteria:

- Pronunciation.
- Fluency.
- Accuracy.
- Vocabulary usage.
- Communication effectiveness.

---

## Reading

Measures written comprehension.

Evaluation criteria:

- Vocabulary recognition.
- Grammar understanding.
- Main idea.
- Supporting details.
- Inference.

---

## Writing

Measures written production.

Evaluation criteria:

- Organization.
- Grammar.
- Vocabulary.
- Clarity.
- Communication objective.

---

## Grammar

Measures practical grammar usage.

Grammar is evaluated through communication tasks rather than isolated rule memorization whenever possible.

---

## Vocabulary

Measures:

- Recognition.
- Recall.
- Contextual understanding.
- Active usage.
- Long-term retention.

---

## Communication

Measures overall ability to complete real-life objectives.

Evaluation criteria include:

- Goal achievement.
- Clarity.
- Adaptability.
- Naturalness.
- Confidence.

---

# Types of Assessment

## Diagnostic Assessment

Completed before beginning the learning journey.

Purpose:

Determine the learner's current proficiency and recommend a starting point.

---

## Formative Assessment

Occurs continuously during learning.

Purpose:

Provide immediate feedback and guide improvement.

---

## Summative Assessment

Completed after finishing lessons, scenarios, or levels.

Purpose:

Measure overall competency development.

---

## Adaptive Assessment

Generated dynamically by the AI Teacher.

Purpose:

Evaluate areas requiring additional evidence before confirming mastery.

---

# Feedback System

Every assessment generates constructive feedback.

Feedback includes:

- Strengths.
- Areas for improvement.
- Grammar observations.
- Vocabulary recommendations.
- Pronunciation feedback.
- Suggested lessons.
- Suggested simulations.
- Suggested Speaking Lab activities.
- Suggested Writing Lab activities.

Feedback should always explain why mistakes occurred and how to improve.

---

# Mastery Levels

Each competency progresses through five levels.

Level 1

Exposure

The learner recognizes the concept.

---

Level 2

Practice

The learner performs successfully with guidance.

---

Level 3

Functional

The learner communicates independently in familiar situations.

---

Level 4

Confident

The learner communicates naturally with only occasional mistakes.

---

Level 5

Mastery

The learner communicates flexibly across different contexts.

# Progress Tracking

Assessment results update:

- Competency Profile.
- Learning Journey.
- Scenario mastery.
- Vocabulary mastery.
- Grammar mastery.
- Overall proficiency.

Historical performance should always remain available for review.

---

# AI Teacher Responsibilities

The AI Teacher should:

- Explain mistakes clearly.
- Encourage learner confidence.
- Recommend personalized review.
- Generate additional practice.
- Adjust future lesson difficulty based on assessment results.

---

# Assessment Principles

Every assessment should be:

- Fair.
- Practical.
- Transparent.
- Communication-focused.
- Personalized.
- Actionable.

Learners should always understand how their performance was evaluated.

---

# Success Definition

The Assessment System succeeds when learners clearly understand their current abilities, recognize their progress, and know exactly what they should improve next to become more competent communicators in Chinese.
