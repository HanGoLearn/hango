# HanGo Lesson Design

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the standard structure of every lesson inside HanGo.

Every lesson follows the same educational framework to ensure consistency, progressive learning, and measurable communication outcomes.

The learner should always know what to expect while still experiencing different situations and challenges.

---

# Lesson Philosophy

Lessons are designed around communication rather than isolated knowledge.

A learner should never feel that they are studying grammar for its own sake.

Instead, every lesson begins with a real-world objective that naturally introduces the language required to accomplish it.

---

# Standard Lesson Flow

Every lesson follows this sequence:

```
Situation

↓

Objective

↓

Dialogue

↓

Comprehension

↓

Vocabulary

↓

Grammar

↓

Guided Practice

↓

Speaking Practice

↓

Writing Practice

↓

AI Challenge

↓

Mission

↓

Reflection
```

---

# 1. Situation

Purpose:

Introduce the learner to a realistic communication context.

The learner immediately understands:

- Where the conversation takes place.
- Who is involved.
- What needs to be accomplished.

Examples:

Restaurant

Airport

University

Business Meeting

Hospital

Hotel

---

# 2. Learning Objective

The lesson clearly states the communication goal.

Example:

After completing this lesson, you will be able to:

- Order food confidently.
- Ask about ingredients.
- Express dietary restrictions.

Objectives must always describe abilities, not knowledge.

---

# 3. Dialogue

The learner first listens to a realistic conversation.

The dialogue should:

- Sound natural.
- Match the learner's level.
- Include target grammar.
- Include target vocabulary.
- Contain authentic expressions.

Resources:

- Native-speed audio.
- Slow-speed audio.
- Transcript.
- Pinyin toggle.
- Translation toggle.

The learner should be encouraged to understand meaning before seeing explanations.

---

# 4. Comprehension

Before studying vocabulary or grammar, learners demonstrate understanding.

Activities may include:

- Multiple-choice questions.
- Matching exercises.
- Ordering events.
- Identifying speaker intentions.
- True or false questions.

The objective is understanding the communication, not memorization.

---

# 5. Vocabulary

Vocabulary is presented within the dialogue.

Each item includes:

- Chinese characters.
- Pinyin.
- Meaning.
- Native pronunciation.
- Example sentence.
- Common collocations.
- Related words.

Vocabulary should always remain connected to context.

---

# 6. Grammar

Grammar explains how communication was achieved.

Each grammar point includes:

- Purpose.
- Structure.
- Usage.
- Examples.
- Common mistakes.
- Comparisons with similar structures.

Grammar explanations should answer:

"Why was this structure used here?"

---

# 7. Guided Practice

Learners immediately apply the new language.

Exercises include:

- Sentence completion.
- Reordering words.
- Controlled substitutions.
- Grammar transformations.
- Contextual translations.

Immediate feedback should be provided.

---

# 8. Speaking Practice

Learners actively produce spoken Chinese.

Activities include:

- Repetition.
- Shadowing.
- Role play.
- Pronunciation practice.
- Guided conversations.

The AI provides pronunciation and fluency feedback.

---

# 9. Writing Practice

Learners reinforce the lesson through writing.

Activities include:

- Creating sentences.
- Completing dialogues.
- Writing messages.
- Short paragraphs.

The AI provides corrections with explanations.

---

# 10. AI Challenge

The AI Teacher creates personalized tasks based on learner performance.

Examples:

- Continue the conversation.
- Solve a new problem.
- Answer unexpected questions.
- Explain your opinion.

Every learner may receive a slightly different challenge.

---

# 11. Mission

The mission simulates a complete real-world interaction.

Examples:

Restaurant:

Successfully order an entire meal while asking about ingredients and making special requests.

Hotel:

Report a room issue and negotiate an acceptable solution.

The mission should integrate all lesson objectives.

---

# 12. Reflection

At the end of the lesson, the learner reviews progress.

Reflection includes:

- What was learned.
- New vocabulary.
- Grammar mastered.
- Communication skills developed.
- Areas for improvement.
- AI recommendations.

The learner leaves the lesson with a clear understanding of what they accomplished.

---

# Lesson Completion Criteria

A lesson is considered complete when the learner has:

- Demonstrated comprehension.
- Used target vocabulary.
- Applied grammar correctly.
- Completed speaking activities.
- Completed writing activities.
- Successfully finished the mission.

---

# AI Teacher Integration

The AI Teacher is available throughout every lesson.

Learners can:

- Ask questions.
- Request examples.
- Generate additional practice.
- Practice conversations.
- Review mistakes.

The AI Teacher always understands the current lesson context.

---

# Lesson Design Principles

Every lesson should be:

- Context-driven.
- Communication-focused.
- Interactive.
- Progressive.
- Practical.
- Personalized.

The learner should finish every lesson feeling more capable of communicating in Chinese than before.

---

# Success Definition

A lesson succeeds when learners gain a new communication ability that they can immediately apply in real life.
