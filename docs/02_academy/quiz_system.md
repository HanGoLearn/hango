# HanGo Quiz System

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The Quiz System provides short, focused assessments that reinforce learning, identify knowledge gaps, and strengthen long-term retention.

Unlike Missions and Simulations, quizzes evaluate specific knowledge and skills in a structured format.

They should be fast, engaging, and educational.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Quizzes are learning tools, not examinations.

Their purpose is to reinforce memory, provide immediate feedback, and prepare learners for real communication.

Every quiz should help learners answer:

"What do I already know, and what should I review next?"

---

# Quiz Characteristics

Every quiz should be:

- Short.
- Focused.
- Interactive.
- Adaptive.
- Contextual.
- Immediately evaluated.

Recommended duration:

3–10 minutes.

---

# Quiz Flow

Every quiz follows the same structure.

```
Introduction

↓

Questions

↓

Immediate Feedback

↓

Score Summary

↓

Review Recommendations

↓

Next Learning Step
```

# Quiz Categories

## Vocabulary Quiz

Evaluates:

- Word recognition.
- Meaning.
- Pronunciation.
- Context.
- Collocations.

Question examples:

- Multiple choice.
- Match the word.
- Complete the sentence.
- Listen and identify.
- Image association.

---

## Grammar Quiz

Evaluates:

- Sentence structure.
- Grammar usage.
- Pattern recognition.
- Error identification.

Question examples:

- Fill in the blank.
- Sentence ordering.
- Error correction.
- Pattern selection.

---

## Listening Quiz

Evaluates:

- General understanding.
- Details.
- Speaker intention.
- Natural-speed comprehension.

Question examples:

- Multiple choice.
- True or false.
- Missing information.
- Sequence events.

---

## Reading Quiz

Evaluates:

- Reading comprehension.
- Vocabulary in context.
- Main idea.
- Supporting details.
- Inference.

---

## Speaking Quiz

Evaluates:

- Pronunciation.
- Fluency.
- Accuracy.
- Confidence.
- Communication clarity.

The AI Teacher analyzes spoken responses and provides personalized feedback.

---

## Writing Quiz

Evaluates:

- Grammar.
- Vocabulary.
- Organization.
- Communication objective.
- Naturalness.

Responses are evaluated by the AI Teacher.

---

## Mixed Quiz

Combines multiple competencies into a single assessment.

Ideal for lesson reviews and unit checkpoints.

---

# Quiz Difficulty

Every quiz is assigned one difficulty level.

## Easy

Recently learned content.

High guidance.

---

## Medium

Mixed review.

Moderate challenge.

---

## Hard

Older content.

Minimal guidance.

Higher communication demands.

---

## Adaptive

Difficulty automatically changes according to learner performance.

---

# Question Types

HanGo supports multiple question formats.

- Multiple choice.
- True or false.
- Matching.
- Drag and drop.
- Fill in the blank.
- Sentence ordering.
- Audio response.
- Voice recording.
- Short answer.
- Paragraph writing.

Each question type should be selected according to the competency being assessed.

---

# Adaptive Quizzes

The AI Teacher personalizes quizzes based on:

- Previous mistakes.
- Weak competencies.
- Review history.
- Lesson completion.
- Vocabulary mastery.
- Grammar mastery.

No two adaptive quizzes are identical.

---

# Feedback

Immediate feedback is provided after every question whenever appropriate.

Feedback includes:

- Correct answer.
- Explanation.
- Related grammar.
- Related vocabulary.
- Common mistakes.
- Suggested review.

Learners should understand why an answer is correct or incorrect.

---

# Scoring

Quiz results include:

- Overall score.
- Accuracy.
- Completion time.
- Competencies evaluated.
- Mastery level.
- Improvement compared to previous attempts.

Scores should emphasize progress rather than perfection.

---

# Quiz Frequency

Quizzes appear:

- During lessons.
- At the end of lessons.
- At the end of scenarios.
- During review sessions.
- Before unlocking advanced content.
- As AI-generated review activities.

The system should avoid excessive repetition.

---

# AI Teacher Responsibilities

The AI Teacher should:

- Explain incorrect answers.
- Generate similar questions.
- Recommend review lessons.
- Adjust future quiz difficulty.
- Encourage continued learning.

---

# Integration With Other Systems

Quiz results update:

- Learning Journey.
- Competency Framework.
- Vocabulary mastery.
- Grammar mastery.
- Progress Dashboard.
- AI personalization.

Quiz performance influences future recommendations but should never permanently block learner progression.

---

# Design Principles

Every quiz should be:

- Fair.
- Clear.
- Fast.
- Motivating.
- Practical.
- Communication-oriented.

Knowledge should always be connected to meaningful language use.

---

# Success Definition

The Quiz System succeeds when learners reinforce previous knowledge, identify weaknesses early, and build confidence through frequent, low-pressure assessments that support long-term language acquisition.
