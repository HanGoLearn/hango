# Purpose

This document defines the brand identity of HanGo.

The objective is to ensure that the product communicates a consistent personality across the application, website, documentation, marketing materials, and future public communication.

HanGo should be recognized as a serious, modern, intelligent, and approachable platform for becoming competent in Chinese.

---

# Brand Name

## HanGo

The name HanGo combines:

- Han, inspired by 汉语, meaning Chinese language.
- Go, representing movement, progress, action, and learning through real use.

The name communicates that learners should not only study Chinese, but actively use it.

HanGo represents progress from knowledge toward communication.

---

# Brand Meaning

HanGo is built around one central idea:

Chinese should be learned through real communication.

The brand should communicate:

- Progress.
- Intelligence.
- Confidence.
- Practical learning.
- Cultural connection.
- Continuous improvement.

HanGo should never feel like a generic flashcard application or a childish language game.

---

# Brand Positioning

HanGo is an AI-powered Chinese learning platform focused on real communicative competence.

It combines:

- Structured learning.
- Real-life scenarios.
- AI tutoring.
- Speaking practice.
- Writing development.
- Competency tracking.

The brand should be perceived as more than an application.

It should feel like a complete Chinese academy with a personal AI teacher.

---

# Brand Promise

HanGo helps learners become capable of using Chinese in real life.

The platform does not promise instant fluency.

It promises:

- A clear learning path.
- Useful and contextual learning.
- Continuous personalized support.
- Measurable communication progress.
- Increasing confidence in Chinese.

# Brand Tone

HanGo communicates in a clear, direct, and encouraging manner.

The tone should be:

- Helpful.
- Respectful.
- Concise.
- Motivating.
- Educational.
- Natural.

The tone should not be:

- Childish.
- Exaggerated.
- Overly playful.
- Mechanical.
- Aggressive.
- Condescending.

---

# Communication Style

HanGo should explain information clearly.

Preferred:

"You are improving your speaking fluency. Practice one more conversation to reinforce this skill."

Avoid:

"Amazing! You are a Chinese superstar! Keep crushing it!"

Preferred:

"This sentence is understandable, but a more natural expression would be..."

Avoid:

"Wrong answer."

The brand should encourage improvement without using artificial enthusiasm.

---

# Brand Voice by Context

## Learning Content

Clear, structured, and educational.

Example:

"This structure is used when the result of an action affects a specific object."

---

## AI Teacher

Supportive, patient, and adaptive.

Example:

"Your answer communicates the idea correctly. Let us improve the word order so it sounds more natural."

---

## Assessment Feedback

Objective and constructive.

Example:

"You completed the mission successfully. Your vocabulary was accurate, but your response became less fluent when explaining the problem."

---

## Progress Messages

Motivating but realistic.

Example:

"You can now handle common restaurant situations independently."

---

## Error Messages

Calm and solution-oriented.

Example:

"We could not process the recording. Check your microphone and try again."

# Visual Personality

The visual identity should communicate:

- Calm focus.
- Educational credibility.
- Technological intelligence.
- Modern simplicity.
- Chinese cultural inspiration without clichés.

The interface should avoid visual overload.

---

# Cultural Direction

HanGo is inspired by Chinese language and culture, but the brand should not depend on stereotypes.

Avoid excessive use of:

- Dragons.
- Lanterns.
- Pandas.
- Traditional red and gold combinations.
- Decorative calligraphy without educational purpose.

Chinese cultural references should be used carefully and meaningfully.

The brand should feel contemporary and international.

---

# Logo Direction

The HanGo logo should be:

- Simple.
- Recognizable.
- Scalable.
- Suitable for mobile app icons.
- Suitable for desktop and web interfaces.
- Legible at small sizes.

Possible directions include:

- A stylized H.
- A symbol combining movement and language.
- A speech-related mark.
- A subtle reference to Chinese characters.
- A minimal symbol representing progression.

The logo should not be overly detailed.

---

# Logo Requirements

The final logo should work in:

- Full color.
- Monochrome.
- Light backgrounds.
- Dark backgrounds.
- Small application icons.
- Website headers.
- Social media profiles.

The logo should remain recognizable without the HanGo wordmark.

---

# Wordmark

The HanGo wordmark should use clear and modern typography.

The visual emphasis may distinguish:

- Han
- Go

However, the word should always be read naturally as HanGo.

The capitalization should remain:

HanGo

Avoid:

- HANGO
- Hango
- hanGO
- hanGo

- Become competent in Chinese.

# Brand Experience

Every interaction with HanGo should reinforce the brand.

The learner should feel:

- The platform understands their level.
- The content has a clear purpose.
- The AI Teacher is genuinely useful.
- Progress is meaningful.
- The interface respects their attention.

The brand is expressed through the complete experience, not only through the logo.

---

# Brand Restrictions

HanGo should not be presented as:

- A quick fluency solution.
- A game for memorizing words.
- A replacement for all human teachers.
- An official HSK institution.
- A guaranteed examination success system.

Claims should remain realistic and defensible.

---

# Brand Success Definition

The HanGo brand succeeds when learners recognize it as a trustworthy, intelligent, and practical platform that helps them become genuinely capable of communicating in Chinese.
