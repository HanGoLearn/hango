# HanGo Success Metrics

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines how HanGo measures success.

Success is not determined solely by user growth or lesson completion.

The primary objective is helping learners develop genuine communicative competence in Chinese.

Every metric should reflect meaningful educational progress.

---

# Educational Success

The most important measure of success is learner improvement.

HanGo succeeds when learners become more capable of using Chinese naturally in real-world situations.

Success includes improvements in:

- Listening comprehension.
- Speaking confidence.
- Reading comprehension.
- Writing ability.
- Pronunciation.
- Grammar accuracy.
- Vocabulary retention.
- Overall communicative competence.

---

# Learning Progress

Learners should experience measurable progress throughout their learning journey.

Examples include:

- Completing increasingly difficult scenarios.
- Using previously learned vocabulary correctly.
- Applying grammar naturally in conversations.
- Producing longer and more complex responses.
- Requiring fewer AI corrections over time.
- Improving pronunciation accuracy.
- Developing greater confidence when speaking.

---

# AI Teacher Success

The AI Teacher is successful when it helps learners progress more efficiently.

Indicators include:

- Answering learner questions clearly.
- Providing useful explanations.
- Delivering personalized recommendations.
- Identifying recurring mistakes.
- Generating relevant additional practice.
- Adapting explanations to each learner.
- Supporting long-term motivation.

The AI Teacher should become increasingly valuable as it learns more about each student.

---

# Platform Success

HanGo should provide a complete and consistent learning experience.

Success includes:

- Smooth navigation.
- Fast performance.
- Consistent educational quality.
- Clear learning progression.
- High-quality AI interactions.
- Reliable speaking and writing evaluation.
- A distraction-free learning environment.

---

# Long-Term Success

Learners should remain engaged over extended periods because they continue to experience meaningful improvement.

The platform should encourage sustainable learning habits rather than short-term activity.

Long-term learners should continuously discover new challenges while reinforcing previously acquired knowledge.

---

# Product Success

HanGo fulfills its purpose when learners can confidently communicate in Chinese across increasingly complex situations.

The platform should produce learners who are capable of:

- Understanding authentic Chinese.
- Participating in natural conversations.
- Writing coherent texts.
- Expressing opinions clearly.
- Communicating in academic and professional environments.
- Continuing their learning independently.

---

# Guiding Principle

Every new feature should answer one question before being implemented:

"Will this improve the learner's ability to communicate in Chinese?"

If the answer is no, the feature should be reconsidered.
