# HanGo Target Users

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

This document defines the users HanGo is designed for.

Understanding our learners allows every product decision to remain focused on solving real educational needs.

HanGo is not intended for everyone.

It is designed for learners who are committed to achieving genuine Chinese proficiency.

---

# Primary Audience

HanGo is designed for learners who already possess basic Chinese knowledge and want to progress toward intermediate and advanced proficiency.

The platform currently focuses on learners studying approximately between HSK 3 and HSK 9 (HSK 3.0).

Learners are expected to already understand basic pronunciation, Pinyin, and fundamental Chinese grammar.

HanGo is not intended to replace beginner courses.

Instead, it helps learners develop the ability to communicate naturally through increasingly complex real-world situations.

---

# Typical Learners

HanGo is designed for learners such as:

- University students.
- Professionals.
- Self-taught learners.
- People preparing to study in China.
- Professionals working with Chinese companies.
- Language enthusiasts seeking long-term fluency.
- Learners preparing for HSK while prioritizing real communication.

---

# Learner Goals

Different learners have different motivations.

HanGo should support goals such as:

- Speaking confidently with native speakers.
- Traveling comfortably in Chinese-speaking regions.
- Studying at Chinese universities.
- Working in international companies.
- Passing HSK examinations.
- Reading books and news in Chinese.
- Watching Chinese media without subtitles.
- Building long-term fluency.

The platform should adapt to these different objectives whenever possible.

---

# Learner Characteristics

HanGo assumes learners are:

Curious.

Motivated.

Willing to study consistently.

Interested in understanding the language rather than memorizing isolated information.

Comfortable using digital learning tools.

Open to interacting with Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Learning Preferences

HanGo prioritizes learners who prefer:

- Structured learning paths.
- Clear explanations.
- Practical examples.
- Continuous feedback.
- Speaking practice.
- Writing correction.
- Real-life conversations.
- Personalized learning.

---

# Language Support

The platform should support multilingual explanations through the AI Teacher.

The learning content remains in Chinese, while explanations may be provided in the learner's preferred language whenever appropriate.

This allows HanGo to support an international audience without changing the educational structure.

---

# Expected Commitment

HanGo is designed for learners who are willing to invest time in long-term improvement.

Progress comes through consistency rather than speed.

The platform encourages regular study habits and continuous practice instead of intensive short-term memorization.

---

# Future Expansion

Although HanGo initially focuses on intermediate and advanced learners, future versions may include complete beginner pathways while maintaining the same educational philosophy.
