# HanGo Values

Version: 1.0

Status: Official

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The following values define the principles that guide every decision made during the design, development, and evolution of HanGo.

Every feature, lesson, interface, AI interaction, and learning experience should reflect these values.

---

# 1. Communication First

Language exists to communicate.

Every lesson, exercise, dialogue, and activity should help learners communicate more naturally in Chinese.

Memorization alone is never the final objective.

---

# 2. Context Before Memorization

Words should never be introduced in isolation.

Vocabulary, grammar, and expressions should always appear inside meaningful situations that reflect real-life communication.

Learners understand better when they know why a word is being used.

---

# 3. Progressive Learning

Learning should feel natural.

New concepts build upon previously acquired knowledge.

Difficulty increases gradually, ensuring learners remain challenged without becoming overwhelmed.

Every lesson should reinforce previous learning while introducing new material.

---

# 4. Personalized Education

Every learner is different.

HanGo adapts to each student's strengths, weaknesses, learning pace, and goals.

The platform should provide personalized recommendations, explanations, and practice whenever possible.

---

# 5. Continuous Practice

Learning does not end when a lesson is completed.

Previously learned vocabulary, grammar, and communication skills should continuously reappear throughout the learning journey.

Long-term retention is more valuable than short-term memorization.

---

# 6. Intelligent Guidance

Artificial Intelligence exists to guide learning.

The AI Teacher should always provide clear explanations, meaningful feedback, encouragement, and personalized support.

AI should simplify learning, not complicate it.

---

# 7. Real-Life Relevance

Every learning activity should prepare learners for situations they may actually experience.

Restaurant conversations.

Travel.

University.

Work.

Daily life.

Professional communication.

Academic discussions.

Chinese should always feel useful.

---

# 8. Clarity Over Complexity

The platform should remain simple, intuitive, and distraction-free.

The learner's attention should stay focused on learning Chinese rather than understanding the interface.

Every design decision should reduce cognitive load.

---

# 9. Consistency

The educational experience should remain consistent across every module.

Learning Journey.

AI Teacher.

Speaking Lab.

Writing Lab.

Progress.

Every module should reinforce the same educational philosophy.

---

# 10. Lifelong Improvement

Language learning is a long-term journey.

HanGo is designed to accompany learners for months and years.

Every interaction should move learners one step closer to genuine communicative competence.

Small improvements accumulate into mastery.

---

# Decision Principle

Whenever multiple design or development choices are possible, the option that better supports these values should always be preferred.
